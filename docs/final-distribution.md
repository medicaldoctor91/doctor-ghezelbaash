## Empirical basis and practical limits

The changes target access, relevant passages, identifiable authorship and specific, attributable clinical information. Their purpose is to increase the opportunity for correct retrieval and citation of the physician's expertise. No ranking improvement is inferred solely from graph size, a machine filename or a mirror distribution.

- [GEO, ACM KDD 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.09735v3) evaluated content interventions on a 10,000-query benchmark and a commercial engine. Citations, quotations and concrete information improved its visibility metrics, with effects varying by domain. Its reported improvements do not constitute a forecast for this physician or today's engines.
- [Ahrefs schema study, May 2026](https://ahrefs.com/blog/schema-ai-citations/) followed 1,885 pages introducing JSON-LD and 4,000 controls. Matched difference-in-differences estimates showed no significant positive citation effect for AI Mode or ChatGPT. Correct markup remains useful for coherent identity and consumers, while content must carry professional meaning itself.
- [Ahrefs llms.txt traffic study, June 2026](https://ahrefs.com/blog/llmstxt-study/) examined 137,210 analytics domains; 97% of published files received no requests in its measured month. This motivates explicit discovery links and usable public content, rather than relying on a special filename. That observational sample does not establish behavior for every crawler.

Actual Search Console inspection confirmed canonical Home and Botox indexing before this release. Those observations establish crawling and canonical ownership, not the cause of rankings or a post-release benefit. Closed search and answer-engine weighting still requires subsequent observation.
