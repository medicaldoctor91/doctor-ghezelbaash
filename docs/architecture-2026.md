# Fixed-corpus architecture decisions

This repository compiles a finite medical/physician corpus into a coherent static distribution. The objective is strong entity identity and meaningful canonical resources with consistent visible, machine and delivery representations; adding schema or URLs is not an objective by itself.

## Canonical surface and primary identity

The reviewed URL policy retains **72 canonical HTML resources**: home plus 71 focused resources. It merges 1,057 old heading/document paths into direct 301 destinations with their original fragments. All 1,455 compiled HTML IDs (1,454 authored IDs plus the main wrapper) have final owners. The policy is explicit in `src/data/url-architecture.json`, validated by `src/lib/url-architecture.mjs`, and consumed by page discovery, reader navigation, redirects and machine citations. A new ID never automatically grants a new indexable URL.

The homepage is both `ProfilePage` and `MedicalWebPage`: it is the primary public physician profile and the entry to his authored clinical portfolio. `/saeed-ghezelbash` redirects to `/#saeed-ghezelbash`. The physician entity keeps the persistent IRI `https://www.ghezelbaash.ir/saeed-ghezelbash`; its navigable `url` is home and `mainEntityOfPage` is `https://www.ghezelbaash.ir/webpage`. Document relocation does not replace the entity's identity.

Every retained topic has substantive initial HTML, a self canonical and contextual links. When a parent contains another retained topic, its focused initial scope omits that child's full text and provides an accessible child link and excerpt. The child owns that text as an indexable resource. The complete page continues to contain the corpus for scrolling and reading; there is no hidden SEO content or user-agent branch.

The scrolling reader preserves that bounded article inside the main landmark. The rest of the corpus remains visible in complementary regions before and after main. Source-derived DOM Range coordinates remove only the primary article's owned source intervals from that surrounding context, including disjoint parent scopes and the bounded historical disclosure. A compiled source signature prevents assembling different cached revisions. Canonical navigation changes the actual primary article as well as its metadata; the full guide does not become every topic's substantive article. This makes primary and contextual content explicit without assuming how a search engine will weight the shared context.

The Arabic, English and Sorani resources are complete equivalent guides, rather than eight separate short question translations. Their hreflang group is reciprocal (`ar-IQ`, `en`, `ku-IQ`) and appears in HTML and sitemap. HTML/RDF preserve Sorani's precise `ckb-IQ`. The broader Persian homepage is not represented as an equivalent translation.

## Sources, graph and supported claims

The HTML body, canonical JSON-LD graph and editorial `llms-guide.md` have separate ownership. URL/rendering/delivery policies select existing facts; they do not become a parallel biography or evidence registry. The build guards these authored files and publishes the complete canonical graph unchanged, while projecting bounded browser graphs for each visible page context.

The replacement graph was challenged against the existing authored corpus. **75 unsupported graph nodes** and associated prestige assertions were removed, including ungrounded qualifications, certifications, awards and auxiliary evidence/metric claims. Supported credentials, explicit identifiers, historical roles, research records and medical relationships remain. This cleanup does not claim that the build independently verified any retained credential or external record.

Six educational procedures retain their clinical concepts and stable IDs but lose unsupported clinic-offering claims: chronic-migraine botulinum toxin, rhinoplasty, surgical facelift, surgical neck lift, generic body aesthetic surgery and hand fat grafting. Their Service/provider/channel, physician availability and catalog memberships were removed together. Actual assessed procedures and diagnosis-based treatment Services remain. Four disease-named Services now explicitly name evaluation/treatment and reference their separate biomedical condition identities.

One source date has one meaning and precision. Typed calendar dates are interpreted correctly by serializers and kept as calendar dates in page projections; revision, review, publication and historical DOI dates are not conflated. The changed current local distribution records, catalog, website and source project record their actual October 4 revision; external releases, unchanged media and medical review dates retain their original dates. No time, timezone, credential or verification event is invented to satisfy a search feature. `ProfilePage` plus a consistent main entity is a semantic statement; rich-result eligibility is not guaranteed.

The authoritative graph defines all 72 canonical documents, with their actual page types, physician relationships, parent/child topology, breadcrumbs and scoped FAQ companions. Downloadable definitions and page-contextual projections are checked against the same document identity contract. Homepage portfolio references do not recursively import every other document's FAQ graph into the homepage.

Focused medical guides retain their visible authored Questions/accepted Answers in bounded FAQ companions. All **125 authored Answer paragraphs**, including safety and specialty/referral boundaries, remain preserved in the comprehensive source. Videos are primary only for real player entry points; a text guide's embedded video is a supporting part. Registry/category relationships retain their relevant concepts without importing entire unrelated catalogs.

## Propagation, delivery and verification

URL decisions propagate to source links, TOC, breadcrumbs, canonicals, sitemap, hreflang, metadata, graph navigational URLs, machine citations, reader routing and HTTP redirects. Entity IRIs are treated separately from public document locations. Retired content paths cannot become canonical targets, and no legacy content alias remains a 200 HTML duplicate.

Browser section navigation retains `/#id` links in the comprehensive reader. Incoming historical `/id` requests independently follow their reviewed 301 decisions to the retained owner and exact fragment. All 1,057 decisions are compiled into HTTP rules; a source-navigation preference cannot silently turn a former public path or the physician IRI into a 404. Live checks compare the build identity in every canonical HTML response, since a fresh deployment manifest cannot detect an older document cached at another URL.

Machine aliases remain legitimate representation endpoints: exact named subjects plus five bounded 200 namespaces (`graph.jsonld`, `provenance.jsonld`, `ontology`, `shapes`, `annotation`). The persistent primary-document IRI `/webpage` also resolves to the authoritative graph as a bounded 200 machine representation. Their MIME, charset, Link and cache headers describe the machine representation at the requested path. Generated Cloudflare rules enforce finite limits and reject ordering/cycle defects; native clean URLs serve the physical canonical HTML.

Direct entry gives meaningful HTML, then automatically expands and positions the same full reader. Native links remain available without JavaScript; failed expansion preserves the topic and offers retry. Metadata and body requests are bounded, and existing media elements retain playback. Real Chromium checks exposed full-document Back restoration being clamped to the short focused HTML without BFCache, reconstructed deferred sections shifting the visible text, and Forward lacking a saved reader position. History now records coordinates plus a stable authored anchor and its viewport offset. Settled scroll tracking measures that reading region, keeps a bounded per-entry memory cache for quick navigation, and throttles native history writes. Restoration uses the saved coordinates to materialize the region, then corrects the anchor after bounded font/layout readiness, only while the entry remains current and neither BFCache nor new reader interaction owns the position. Old snapshots retain coordinate fallback; unchanged fragment navigation and entries without a valid saved position keep native restoration. Performance optimizations preserve content, entity signals, responsive media sizing, layout stability and crawlable links. Multilingual disclosure headings avoid the unrelated large chapter-start margin inside their summaries.

Regression checks audit URL/anchor coverage, final link destinations, graph/entity consistency, browser language context, date precision, resource MIME types and machine alias delivery. The normal build validates the generated distribution itself, writes a schema inventory for its 72 canonical resources and records file hashes. Live deployment status, cache/redirect/CSP/compression behavior and mobile/desktop rendering require separate checks against the deployed commit; search indexing and entity recognition remain decisions of external systems.

The old standalone SHACL file contradicted the replacement graph, and generated provenance dates had lost their RDF datatypes. The distribution now compiles all graph-authored SHACL constraints together with scoped supplemental rules; medical concept categories do not imply clinic service providers, and external archives do not acquire invented local checksums. Published provenance preserves the exact recorded date/dateTime literal. Actual SHACL and Meta-SHACL validation cover the final graph/DCAT/provenance union; CI also rejects six identity, evidence, date, subject and checksum mutations.
