# Historical evidence index

The dated folders in this directory preserve observations, migrations, test outputs and publication receipts from their recorded runs. They are historical evidence, not a statement of current public state. A successful old test or a report that names an older DOI/revision remains scoped to that original snapshot.

Do not globally replace historical DOIs, author names, source commits, timestamps or row counts. Follow each report's own run/source identifiers. The current frozen candidate and its exact artifact/hash are documented in [the maintenance checkpoint](../docs/maintenance-release.md); later live observations must be recorded separately.

- `doi-policy/`: DOI occurrence and role inventories from their recorded source.
- `positioning-restoration-20261019/`: dated preservation, migration, packaging and platform observations. The original messages and evidence retain their historical roles.
- Other dated directories and release/audit receipts: consult the file's recorded run, source and observation date before using any conclusion.

The cleanup inventories under `docs/maintenance/` describe Git ref retention only. They are not dataset publication receipts and do not change the frozen payload.
